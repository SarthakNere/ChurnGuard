Evaluation note

These results reflect the project's recorded evaluation. They have not been independently audited. A stronger production assessment would include cross-validation, threshold selection using validation data, leakage checks, calibration analysis and evaluation on newer customer data. The dataset's churn distribution is descriptive and is not evidence of the model's predictive accuracy.
